Privacy Policy

Plain and simple: I respect your privacy. Here's what happens with any information you share through this website.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

1. Information I Collect

When you contact me through the contact form, WhatsApp, or email, you may share your name, email address, phone number, and project details. That's it — I don't collect anything else, and there are no accounts or sign-ups on this site.

2. How I Use It

Your information is used for one purpose only: to respond to your inquiry and discuss your project. I don't sell, rent, or share your details with anyone, and I don't add you to any marketing list.

3. How the Contact Form Works

The contact form on this site doesn't store your message on any server — when you hit send, it opens WhatsApp with your message pre-filled, and the message goes directly to me through WhatsApp. Standard WhatsApp and email privacy policies apply to those conversations.

4. Cookies & Tracking

This website doesn't use analytics, advertising trackers, or third-party cookies. The only cookies that may exist are strictly functional ones (for example, caching that keeps the site fast). If I ever add analytics in the future, this policy will be updated first.

5. Third-Party Links

This site links to external services — LinkedIn, GitHub, WhatsApp, and client websites. I don't control those sites, so their own privacy policies apply once you leave here.

6. Your Rights

You can ask me at any time what information I hold about you, or ask me to delete it. Just email asad.aiworks@gmail.com and I'll take care of it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "last updated" date.
